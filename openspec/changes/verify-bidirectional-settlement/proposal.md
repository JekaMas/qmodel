## Why

Both banks can issue or hold securities and payment tokens. A single fixed placement cannot exercise concurrent trades that assign opposite roles to the same physical ledger.

## What Changes

Specify both cross-ledger directions and both local placements. Execute them on one shared native account model, including proof delays, short funding, retries and simultaneous decisions on different hosts.

## Impact

Extends the Quint verification and the PoC architecture. Bank approval of finality, deployment and token conformance remain external.
