# Design

Use a finite native-hold core plus evidence, operational/control, relational privacy and timed resource models. Reuse descriptors and economic contracts across A/B/D variants. Preserve every counterexample before any repair. Run smoke controls first, then core witnesses/simulation/bounded/finite/temporal checks, then required variants, trust, privacy, timing and integrated configurations.

Models are abstractions; only checked mappings establish refinement, and safety mappings do not preserve liveness automatically. Candidate sources and model/tool revisions are identified in verification/sources and results/manifest.json. Full v2 scope remains NOT DONE until every mandatory suite has an executed result or a precise visible blocker.

## Independent review iteration

Review starts from exact base 4e3b8d0 and the original source request, not the author's asserted result. One reviewer retains IR-001–IR-004 through repair/recheck. Root owns code/spec/results and publication; reviewer owns independent-review.md/json. Privacy payload provenance is internal, while complete wire responses enter the relational trace. Historical model greens are retained when unchanged; comment-only deltas are explicitly recorded. New execution force, exact receipt selection, immutable native exports and the sealed proof contract prevent the demonstrated reproduction/audit defects. The seal is versioned local evidence, not third-party attestation.

Timing/capacity scopes remain separate: two-trade round-robin timing versus finite hostile FIFO service, with no invented priority or combined deadline theorem. Scope/failure controls are retained as results. Publication occurs only after reviewed repairs, current evidence, final clean-revision gate and remote readback. No archive, deployment or additional paid calls is implied.
