# Staged fault and race plan

The [settlement specification](../../../verification/SPECIFICATION.md) defines the protocol stages. This matrix distinguishes a concrete executed check in the new N3 profile, a retained scope from another profile, and an open model or implementation obligation. A representative fault trace does not exhaust every ordering, parameter or cross-product.

| Stage | Ordinary and Byzantine faults to cover | Race and linearization requirement | Check and disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Request and authorization | Delay, duplicate, prior epoch, same ID/different terms, missing signer, malicious party or unauthorized submitter, conflicting recipient, quantities, asset, ledger or contract | Each authenticated signed descriptor authorizes one identity. An early signature or transport accept cannot allocate an asset. | Existing descriptor field and role witnesses. N3 exact foreign proof, unknown trade and invalid outcome. Real encodings, cryptography and idempotency store pending at the PoC. |
| Admission, rotation and revocation | Interrupted membership update, removed institution, expired credential, stale governance response, attacker-chosen membership, premature retirement | Revoke-before-reserve denies. Reserve-before-revoke retains an accepted obligation. Epoch transition must preserve pending historical witnesses and deny new authority from an obsolete key. | Both admission orderings in the new matrix. Retain membership and trust rotation/retirement controls. Any joint validator reconfiguration with ongoing independent settlement at arbitrary schedules is open. |
| Reserve on A, prepare on B | Provisioning failure, both leg orders, oversubscription, ordinary spend race, local downtime, pause, seizure, double use, unsigned or malicious authority | Each reservation linearizes at its ledger mutation after authorization and local capacity checks. Debit/spend includes active holds in unavailable capacity. B may prepare before A knows it. | Both TLC N3 capacity profiles and directed third rejection. Retain old frozen/revoked eligibility, spend, late prepare, expiry, escrow, seizure and non-atomic controls. External asset/issuer powers remain ledger assumptions. |

Arbitrary clock drift and consensus mempool/replacement ordering require additional semantics. |

Conflicting roots must not both authorize the same fixed context under the stated quorum premise. | New fixed four-validator, threshold-three envelope with at most one Byzantine. | Apply on B | Stopped/frozen B, unavailable submitter, COMMIT proof in-flight, repeated apply, replay on wrong hold, authentic wrong phase, overwrite of known outcome, forged asset/recipient or double transfer | B application linearizes when its active cash hold is consumed under a locally verified COMMIT. ## Execution and widening

1. Run the native N3 decision graphs with two and three funded pairs. Confirm the nonvacuous reachability of three reservations separately. These use the genuine primitive job actions with the scope ending at the decision.
2. Run the fault matrix and the direct combined scenario. The route/fault environment allows 1, 2, 3, 5 or beyond-horizon duration, four outage or partition starts and four injected candidates, with a bound of eight elapsed ticks. A/B blocks are explicit choices. Ordinary signs, local trade actions, historical proof jobs, loss and prune each have an actual action. Run seeded safety samples over this relation.
3. Check N3 evidenced completion after actual mixed outcomes with one optional two-tick interruption to B, prover or transport. Require stable availability and per-trade work fairness. Compare the theorem with a positive complete-state witness. This exhaustive suffix and the wider sampled relation are different proof scopes.
4. Execute the fixed Byzantine envelope, deliberate quorum/context controls, deadline/submitter/revoke races, protected/unprotected queue and actual SQLite invocation/response checks. Preserve failing tool runs and native counterexamples.
5. Keep open model work visible. The next independently specifiable products are repeated timed recovery plus hostile finite work queues, alternative placement and economic authority fault products, bounded clock-rate drift with quantitative validity, and compositional proofs beyond three trades. Concrete networks, contracts, verifier, keys, backups, banking and clock/SLA measurement are tracked in [the PoC conformance cases](../../../verification/reports/guarantee-conformance.md). Each open product has to preserve the actual hold, canonical authority and reporting contract and specify its own reachability, safety, bounded/conditional liveness and negative controls.

## Source-to-check decisions

- **Transaction decision.** Gray and Lamport separate coordinator decision and participant outcome. Trace the local A atomic effect and remote known/applied stages separately. [Consensus on Transaction Commit](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/video/consensus-on-transaction-commit.pdf) · [Executable TwoPhase TLA+](https://github.com/tlaplus/Examples/blob/master/specifications/transaction_commit/TwoPhase.tla)
- **Linearization.** Herlihy and Wing compare observed concurrent invocation/response histories to a sequential object preserving completed-before-invoked order. An incomplete call may be needed to explain an observed effect. The SQLite object checks this exact condition on captured histories. [Linearizability paper](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~wing/publications/HerlihyWing90.pdf) · [SQLite atomic commit](https://www.sqlite.org/atomiccommit.html)
- **Time and Byzantine authority.** Partial synchrony distinguishes the stabilization premise from quantitative pre-stabilization delay. PBFT motivates the quorum intersection, while the Tendermint light-client specification motivates source trust and epoch checking separately from execution. [Partial synchrony](https://courses.csail.mit.edu/6.897/fall04/papers/Dwork/consensus-in-ps.pdf) · [PBFT](https://www.usenix.org/legacy/publications/library/proceedings/osdi99/full_papers/castro/castro_html/castro.html) · [Tendermint light client](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.07031)
- **Transport, restart, resource faults.** IBC distinguishes packet sequence, timeout, receipt and ack. FoundationDB documents deterministic campaigns with reproduced seeds, multiple failure levels and severe load. The Builders' Library distinguishes retrying the same intent from replacing it under the same ID. The settlement source permits B release upon authenticated A ABORT. [ICS-004](https://github.com/cosmos/ibc/blob/main/spec/core/ics-004-channel-and-packet-semantics/README.md) · [FoundationDB testing](https://github.com/apple/foundationdb/blob/main/documentation/sphinx/source/testing.rst) · [Idempotent APIs](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/)
- **Quint evidence.** Retain source-bound directed traces and independent event coverage by stage, then separate exact state enumeration from seeded exploration. [Quint checkers](https://quint.sh/docs/model-checkers) · [Malachite Quint coverage](https://github.com/circlefin/malachite/blob/a453b32155f4ba9f959724279b52f601bae92e09/specs/consensus/quint/coverage.md)

The [funded-block change](../settle-funded-blocks/tasks.md) adds the same-height case. Otherwise eligible requests that fit the block's full demand all succeed, independent of local order. Oversubscription and genuine deadline expiry have separate checks.

## Open proof products and completion conditions

This table records the original handoff. The [logical-protocol disposition](../../../verification/reports/proof-products.md) covers the seven products under the subsequently clarified contract-and-action scope and records the remaining proof boundaries.

| Product | Model or implementation increment | Independent discriminator |
|---|---|---|
| Sustained end-to-end hostile work | Compose causal capture/prove/inclusion/retry queues with the N3 hold state, retain ready obligations across restart and measure bounded service after stabilization. Include hostile request IDs, duplicate arrivals and preemption. | Shared slot exhaustion, admission/priority inversion and missing recovery service must give a missed bound. Verify safety separately at larger or permanent workloads. |
| Variable chain and elapsed rates | Add independent block rates, clock offset and changing drift, then give confirmation/evidence issuance an explicit layer with bounds and replacement. Quantify assumptions by source and route. | Late COMMIT vs ABORT split must fail if a B wall clock is substituted. A slow or halted A must expose where elapsed-time promises lose their premise. |
| Change of Byzantine authority over time | Refine the fixed certificate scope with rounds, locks, predecessor-authorized membership, historical snapshots and mobile corruption. Connect accepted certificate to actual canonical execution and proof statement. | Equivocation at same height across views, just-retired authority on new work, delayed genuine old certificate and incompatible checkpoint recovery must distinguish the bound. |
| Other placement and trust products | Use the A and B/D/share placement tables and retain local consumption differences. Compose funded multi-trade scheduling, retained history, partial loss, correlated fault occurrences and honest-member scope per placement. | A false transfer without its local COMMIT effect, inconsistent remote outcomes and premature/forged completion must violate independent source-effect/record checks. |
| On-chain invocation and storage recovery | Install actual request/transaction/status adapters in the two-network PoC. Instrument invocation, accepted inclusion, canonical finality, source effect, retry and response. Inject true contract rollback and worker/database restore events. | Compare to sequential local objects and real ledger/DB effects. Assert no early acknowledgement, partial debit, stale-authority lookup, hidden duplicate or unrecorded ledger effect. |
| End-to-end observable privacy | Use paired histories under common public requests, including protocol outages, status polling, transaction batches, proof/backup metadata and entitled role changes. | Deliberate leaked bounds, identity via routing, stale responder scope and lost key cases must change the right observation without being hidden by a cache or omitted trace. Existing finite privacy theorems retain their narrower scopes. |
| Arbitrary population and cross-stage composition | Generalize the funding, deadline, UTXO/hold and authenticated event identity interfaces; use exact small instances plus independently proved inductive preservation and named refinements. | An implementation that accidentally aliases late and old identities, changes same-pair capacity, or consumes a hold more than once must violate across the chosen population. Sampled higher counts are exploratory; only a parameterized proof closes the unbounded claim. |
