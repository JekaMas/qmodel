## Design

Generate isolated, source-bound three-trade instances of the existing A-hosted model. The two capacity settings fund three or two pairs. The finite admission-to-decision selector chooses all trades and primitive actions in its declared scope. The more expansive profile keeps independent elapsed time, availability windows, partitions, ordinary recovery and bounded adversarial actions. It checks the actual settlement core with existing outcome and record monitors. It also observes real prior state, deadline and output identity.

Keep custody and execution faults separate from the baseline admission assumptions. Check explicit Byzantine certificate and history controls with expected verdicts. Local financial actions have their own linearization points. Responses, relays, stale status and global economic usability are distinct observables. See the companion [fault and race matrix](fault-matrix.md) for stages, proof scope and evidence.
