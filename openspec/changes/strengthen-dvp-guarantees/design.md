## Architecture

Keep the existing State, init and transition relation for the principal proof profiles. Add a layer of active assertions over the same state, with host-specific local and remote leg rules. The assertions can inspect source history. Consumers continue to rely on locally accepted authenticated facts. A proof that establishes preparation never authorizes a terminal application.

The evidence-only reporting projection queries matching application evidence. A separate source-world oracle checks that those facts correspond to real economic effects. Durable COMMIT rows remain subject to this oracle even when crash resets the report cache.

L04 already checks eventual booking and a report of at least 2 under the recorded per-action fairness assumptions. On the same graph, an invariant that its endpoint implies matching terminal outcome, correct row and actual evidenced completion strengthens the eventual endpoint by logical implication. Monitor this implication explicitly. No fairness or proof-job restriction is introduced.

A narrow real SQLite adapter demonstrates durable idempotent booking, restart and stale backup restore against retained authoritative evidence fixtures. The adapter receives records after a caller-supplied authentication boundary. Concrete QBFT, circuits, Solidity and downstream banking integration require their own subjects. Pair the executable adapter case with explicit conformance vectors for these remaining PoC obligations.
