The multilateral installation has three independent availability/height domains, two security sources (0/2), a shared cash source (1), authenticated domain-bound outcomes, separate reservations and B-local terminal knowledge. Its separate progress theorem includes per-trade application/refund and COMMIT reporting.

The HTLC representative gives the secret to the buyer, an A claim deadline 3 and B deadline 6, with explicit observation/inclusion margins. The narrow root/signalling representative omits arbitrary nested call trees, dynamic contract graphs and vendor recovery details. The committee comparison authenticates a remote fact under an honest threshold and separately breaks it under a compromised threshold; a mandatory committee can still block availability even with additional proof checks.
