## Research decisions

| Prior comparison | Adopted in this change | Remaining decision or dependency |
|---|---|---|
| Gray–Lamport transaction commit and certified deals | Keep one authoritative decision and check each leg against it. Keep decision, local application and the reporting projection observable separately. | Paxos Commit or a certified decision ledger D would change authority and proof paths. Neither removes the dependency on local asset availability. |
| HTLC analysis | Keep the exclusive deadline on A and authenticated outcomes. The wrong-phase proof and historic late-proof cases exercise authorization separately from timing. | HTLC is a comparison with different hashlock and chain-progress assumptions. |
| Tendermint light client and model-based testing | Bind claimed terminal/application facts to the assumed canonical history and admitted historical program; compare actual outputs against independent input and state. | Implement and verify the QBFT/circuit/encoding boundary on actual networks. Model authenticity is symbolic. |
| GPACT | A two-leg kernel uses one remote preparation and one remote outcome path. Reconciliation is separate. Repeated delivery does not rerun the transfer. | Generic call trees and additional decision authorities need separate requirements. |
| Canton and Stella | Keep the native-ledger and shared-execution variants separate. | The native configuration still admits a period of independent local usability. A simultaneous-use requirement would require a shared execution domain or a separately enforced and verified entitlement policy. |

The [verified protocol comparison](../reviews/guarantee-assurance/README.md#comparison-with-verified-work) gives the cited primary results and their limits. The [conformance cases](guarantee-conformance.md) specify observations for actual finality, local transfer rollback, upgrades, retained evidence, integrated booking, private usability and cost/latency. Broader combined membership liveness, hostile-work timing, arbitrary populations and cryptographic/deployed guarantees remain open at those boundaries.

Run `python3 -B verification/scripts/guarantee_audit.py` to check the [exact assurance selection](guarantee-validation.json). The full acceptance audit includes these observations and keeps historical timeouts/tool errors separate. The four new directed discriminator cases are passing *witness tests of faulty producers*. The two policy-history controls are actual native counterexamples. Neither kind counts as a green safe baseline. The first QNT parser attempts are preserved as non-successes. The earlier valid SQLite receipt covers the earlier input shape; the selected receipt adds exact integer-type rejection.
