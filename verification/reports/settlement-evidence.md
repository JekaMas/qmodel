# Settlement, recovery and conformance evidence

Final COMMIT irrevocably delivers the security to the buyer. The cash stays reserved for the seller. Once the cash ledger recovers, stays online and services the pending payment, the seller receives the full amount exactly once. Quint verifies this in both directions. The banks’ settlement rules determine whether COMMIT or final cash credit discharges the buyer’s debt. [Two-direction recovery scenarios](../tests/routed_settlement_test.qnt) · [900-state safety and liveness check](../results/runs/admitted-routes-recovery-attempt1/raw.log)

If the cash ledger stays offline for a week, the seller’s native cash balance still awaits credit. The model sets no deadline for payment. An enforceable claim or alternate payout during the outage needs bank rules and operational evidence. Simultaneous final native transfers require common execution or a demonstrated equivalent. [Bank decision](../configs/bank-finality.json)

## Verification evidence

The subsequent [guarantee validation](guarantee-validation.md) independently checks terminal outcome authority, evidence-backed COMMIT completion, previous-state admission and actual terminal immutability. The safety and conditional liveness results apply to the specified finite configurations. Its transition relation adds B/prover outages and B freeze/unfreeze to the settlement step. Apalache also completed [reference safety through depth 12](../results/runs/qa-apalache12/receipt.json). The [specification](../SPECIFICATION.md#three-distinct-guarantees) maps all five properties to assertions, guards and temporal formulas.

The model permits A to record a signed tombstone from NONE and B to learn ABORT while no hold exists. Delayed and repeated delivery are modeled. [Directed network scenarios](../results/runs/qa-scenarios-network_test/receipt.json) exercise message loss/resend, lost acknowledgement and A halt/recovery. The recovery theorem requires an eventually stable all-up and unfrozen suffix, durable evidence and weak fairness for each required action. It permits resolution by ABORT and has no elapsed-time bound.
