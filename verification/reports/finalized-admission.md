## Where the finality guarantee ends

At final A COMMIT the buyer can receive the security while B is halted. The matching B cash remains encumbered. The seller’s cash balance has not increased. Repeating idle preserves this state. After recovery, presenting the terminal proof pays the reserved amount once. The same two transfers occur in one model transition when both assets share an execution ledger. Moving the decision to an independent D leaves the local application interval. [Halt and recovery scenarios](../tests/bank_finality_test.qnt) · [Shared execution](../tests/mechanisms_test.qnt)

Ask both banks to identify the beneficiary, amount, responsible debtor, legal point of irrevocability and treatment of insolvency or intervention, plus the route by which the seller can obtain payment during a prolonged B outage. If an external route pays, its evidence must prevent double payment on B recovery. If only final credit on B constitutes payment, strict DvP requires common atomic execution or another demonstrated equivalent. No bank response is recorded. The executable results address reservation and delivery, and the PoC needs real per-ledger cutoff checkpoints, verifier retention, QBFT, contracts, circuits and bank integration. [Two-network acceptance cases](guarantee-conformance.md)

The bank gate reflects the distinction between a final settlement and the technical delivery of a message. CPMI-IOSCO Principle 8 addresses the clarity and certainty of the moment of settlement. Principle 12 conditions the final settlement of each linked obligation on the other. The applicability and contractual meaning for these assets require the banks’ assessment. [PFMI principles 8 and 12](https://www.bis.org/committees/cpmi/pfmi/overview)

## Verification and reuse

The [current index](../configs/admission-acceptance.json) selects 168 native observations. These comprise 29 successful exhaustive TLC checks, 21 expected native TLC violations, 101 directed executions covering 779 named cases, and 17 simulations covering 23,600 bounded traces. The 216 new observation records also preserve development attempts. Only the explicitly selected executions contribute to these totals. The audit regenerates workload sources, compares command, scope and input hashes, checks raw verdicts and verifies the names of the directed cases. VGR-01 and VGR-02 were evaluated again on nine current graphs, including 684,268 states for two contending trades and 125,632 states with recovery. Seven current temporal profiles were independently checked and paired with the same financial source, initial state and transition selection for the strengthened L04 endpoint. Shared and private profiles in this selection supply safety evidence. The full two-trade progress graph reached depth 49 with an empty exploration queue. [Two-trade native result](../results/runs/admitted-live-two/raw.log) · [Recovery native result](../results/runs/admitted-live-recovery/raw.log)

The separate three-trade recovery graph checked 856,074 reachable states after a real COMMIT/ABORT/COMMIT prefix. After eventual restoration and per-trade fair service all three reach evidenced completion. Each negative TLC control requires a native violation of its selected property. The N7 binding matrix has 76 changed candidate inputs and one unchanged phase-6 statement, exercised both without proof and as a genuine idempotent replay.

The historical 367 selections keep the source identity of the [reviewed release](https://github.com/JekaMas/qmodel/commit/116bef8e682acaf5fe48865a0463259e1a8d7795). The audit checks those against their original Git tree and confirms that preserved model/verification owners still match. Re-executions on revised sources have separate receipt IDs. Sample counts denote bounded traces and directed cases denote particular histories. None of those counts measure independent properties, a time bound or operational availability. In the recorded runs, the generic human-readable resource label came from the older runner default. The saved TLC configuration and native startup log identify the actual heap and worker count. Independent jobs sometimes ran concurrently, so wall times are unsuitable for benchmarking. The runner now labels fresh invocations from their selected config.
