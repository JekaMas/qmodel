Both ledgers and their adapters are modeled through logical contracts and actions. The [machine-readable results](proof-products-results.json) select 43 exact receipts by ID and distinguish exhaustive checks, directed scenarios, seeded runs and symbolic obligations. ## Parameterized economic theorem

For any positive finite population, any positive reservation amounts, and nonnegative backing capacities, each of the 17 local transition equations preserves the row invariants and the two global capacity/supply balances. Affected and observer indices are arbitrary. The array point update changes only the selected row. For each ledger, aggregate reservation after the update is the old sum minus the affected row's former active allocation plus its new active allocation. The solver proves the inequality and conservation with that substitution. Induction from the all-empty rows also preserves terminal immutability, single hold consumption, agreement of local terminal knowledge and sufficient proof-backed preparation, and the release/application and booking markers. With phases 0–6 the symbolic encoding `7 × trade + phase` is injective for positive integer trade IDs.

This is a theorem of the extracted economic equations conditional on authentic authorization. The source correspondence monitor reads actual receipts, balances and all trade rows before and after the observed primitive and checks the guard, exact update and unchanged observers. It exercises initialization, ordinary spend, reservation, remote registration, proof delivery, commit/abort, learning, both effects, release and reporting through real source actions in the selected profiles. A universal refinement of the entire executable fault, privacy, ledger and time relation is still open.

## Scope and tradeoffs

- The job profile checks an already admitted N3 batch, one loss and one outage. Its 56-slot bound is in eligible grants after recovery. Continuous unbounded admission, arbitrary repeated resets and an elapsed-time bound across both chains are outside that proof. Missing permanent service does not authorize an unsafe result.
- The authority model checks a fixed honest-lock rule and one predecessor-authorized handoff. It assumes honest final-vote discipline and authenticated statement construction. The intentionally mobile-key case shows why limiting simultaneous corruption alone is insufficient. Progress through competing proposals, arbitrary membership histories and a concrete consensus implementation require different evidence.
- Placement and contract-history tests cover the named races and logical effects. Timed faults, privacy and all placements do not form one exhaustive combined graph. Shared execution changes the custody/execution premise. A separate decision ledger adds another authority and both remote application paths.
- Two-world safety is conditional on the modeled observer, coupled public schedule and ideal hiding. Cryptographic confidentiality, arbitrary scheduling and real side channels have separate proof obligations.

## Architecture decision

Retain A as the reference decision owner. The TLA+ transaction-commit comparison motivates agreement over a stable decision and separate resource-manager application. Replicating the coordinator or moving it to D changes fault tolerance and governance while retaining both native asset availability requirements. In the verified D placement, the two preparation proofs and the two remote applications make the additional dependencies explicit. Shared execution is simpler for simultaneous effects only when both assets can actually execute in that domain. The Quint ICS20 comparison motivates exact post-effect recipient and amount checks. Its packet timeout and acknowledgement conditions differ from this protocol's exclusive A deadline, so B's wall clock supplies no refund authority.
