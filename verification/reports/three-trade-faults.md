# Three-trade fault and race verification

The [stage matrix](../../openspec/changes/check-three-trade-faults/fault-matrix.md) maps ordinary and Byzantine faults to evidence or an explicit open boundary. The matrix follows the protocol stages, Quint actions and listed specifications. The [acceptance ledger](three-trade-faults.json) binds observed results to source and command. The wider review asks which faults can occur at each stage and which mutation or guard distinguishes them.

## Same-block guarantee

The earlier admission/decision graph's temporal target allows either COMMIT or ABORT when inclusion is unconstrained. The [funded-block profiles](funded-blocks.md) separately check all local orders when included requests fit the spendable balance, including completing all three COMMITs at the last legal A height. Genuine partial funding has its own A-only and B-only profiles.

## Interpretation

The three-trade specialization uses per-trade deadlines, capacity accounting and a one-to-one symbolic root over the selected trade and phase domains. Its substitution chooses a different existing trade and also has a separate nonexistent ID case. Recognized outcome/leg domains, spending while down and route effects are guarded. The graph does not grant custody or visibility of other live ledger state to the actual actor primitives. Schedulers select the next modeled action. Each local action uses its own guard.

A decision with security delivery on A, cash hold consumption on B, releases, and the SQLite booking have different linearization points. The observed histories compare invocation/response precedence to a single sequential **booking** object. Claiming one linearization point for simultaneous economic usability on autonomous ledgers would contradict existing counterexamples.

The fixed-context certificate envelope assumes the signed statement and authentic validator identities. The two-outcome recovery theorem relies on the named suffix, retained evidence and stated fairness. The bounded hostile result counts eligible service ticks after recovery of an already-authorized application path. Additional proof-worker overload, repeated jointly Byzantine outage products, cryptography, actual QBFT/contract behavior, application availability deadlines and legal/production conformance remain open obligations in the [stage matrix](../../openspec/changes/check-three-trade-faults/fault-matrix.md).

The receipt selection preserves initial non-successes. Contention first needed a clock frame in its denial test. Certificate scopes first needed a local name outside the built-in namespace. The queue witness was strengthened to require actual saturation **before** both service ticks, so its previous observations remain separate. The optional second TLC reachability search was interrupted after the exact directed witness of the same bootstrap and work actions had passed. The completed 856,074-state temporal result is separate.
