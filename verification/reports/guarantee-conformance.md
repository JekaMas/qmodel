# Two-network PoC conformance cases

Run these cases when the A/B contracts, verifier and bank integration are available. Use the signed descriptor, finalized source transaction/block IDs and both ledgers' before/after balances, holds, decisions and emitted application events as the comparison inputs. Save raw traces and the exact contract, verifier and configuration identities. An API acknowledgement or an adapter cache cannot establish a financial effect.

| Case | Setup and action | Required observations | Current evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terminal meaning | B prepares first. A reaches the deadline and finalizes an ABORT tombstone. Present that valid proof to the COMMIT consumer. Then present it to the ABORT consumer. | Wrong-phase request leaves B's cash, hold and accepted outcome unchanged. The valid ABORT path releases exactly that hold. Alter the trade, recipient, source genesis and checkpoint one at a time. Each mismatched input leaves both ledgers unchanged. | Quint review control and [wrong-phase assertion](../results/runs/guarantees-control-wrong-phase/receipt.json). Actual QBFT and verifier pending. |
| Local transfer rollback | Both holds are ready before the deadline. Exercise a reverting transfer, a false return, a short transfer and a reentrant token at A's COMMIT boundary. Then retry with an admitted working asset configuration. | A has no committed terminal decision, transfer, debit, application event or proof of COMMIT from the failed transaction. The security stays reserved. Successful retry atomically binds the decision to the full security delivery. | Quint freeze and retry plus omitted-delivery control. Actual token/EVM behavior pending. |
| Late proof and deadline | Delay a finalized B preparation until the deadline, race it with the tombstone, and present it again after ABORT. Test the block immediately before the deadline as a control. | A at height D can ABORT. A at height D−1 can COMMIT with the two valid obligations. Late historical evidence cannot overwrite ABORT. B can apply or release only on matching A evidence. | Original Quint deadline/orphan race receipts. Native-inclusion and receipt canonicalization pending. |
| Rotation, exit, and intervention | Prepare both legs. Rotate from a predecessor-authorized checkpoint and exit the admitted member. Unfreeze and replay historical proofs, including an orphan ABORT case. | Old admitted authority resolves the retained obligation. New admission and unadmitted proof are rejected. | Report and restore | Wait for only A application. Remove, replace or delay B evidence. Snapshot the empty projection. Finish on B, ingest the two complete finalized application records, restore the older projection, restart independently, and replay both proof results in duplicate/reverse order. | No completed row with one proof or a mismatched leg. After actual application, the rebuilt row binds the exact descriptor and two source event IDs. Each ledger shows one effect and the external bank system books once. If the required path is irrecoverable, label pending/unknown or delivered-without-usability. An independent post-commit freeze/seizure power must be represented by the product's real asset terms. | Separate Quint trust, privacy and intervention models. Real verifier, confidentiality, usability and asset rights pending. |
| Capacity and freshness | On a defined workload vary proof batching, relative block/proof rates and recovery traffic. Withhold a newer head from the reporting endpoint. | Record observed distribution of time to decision, both applications and booking, age of outstanding holds, recovery traffic waits and proof costs. Report as-of and unknown freshness based on authenticated source progress. | Reference finite slot, capacity and omission models. Production availability, service limits and cost pending. |

The SQLite exercise assumes an upstream authenticator has already established the evidence's provenance and durable event identity. It checks binding and persistence in the reference A-hosted COMMIT projection. ABORT and private note/key behavior remain modeled and have separate external test cases. See the [general external obligations](conformance-boundary.md).


## Conditions for the two-network PoC

1. Obtain each bank’s rule for discharge of the buyer’s debt at final COMMIT or final cash credit. For recognition at COMMIT during a week-long cash-ledger halt, identify the seller’s irrevocable entitlement to the specific reserved funds, responsible debtor, treatment under insolvency/freeze, executable payment route, time conditions and replay-safe reconciliation on recovery. A-hosted remains a PoC pending both banks’ rules. Repeat the halt in both physical directions. After stable recovery, submit the pending outcome and a duplicate. Confirm the seller’s native balance increases by the full signed amount once and the reserve is consumed. Quint already checks this recovery guarantee. This exercise tests the real networks and adapter. If simultaneous final native transfers are required, qualify common atomic execution or an equivalent. [Model evidence](../results/runs/admitted-routes-recovery-attempt1/raw.log)
2. Rotate the two networks with authenticated per-source cutoff checkpoints. Try signatures alone, a submitted reservation, two unfinalized reservations, delayed delivery of a genuine pre-cut reservation from each source, an unseen retired-validator certificate and new credentials. Retain the finalized identifiers, epoch, signed terms, beneficiary and both balance deltas.
3. Stop block production on A while advancing B and wall time; submit and replace a request. Inspect provisional inclusion, finality depth, spendability and timers separately. Restart each consumer independently with an older cache. Compare its latest and explicit as-of responses with authenticated prefixes. Cover withheld heads and one-recipient delivery.
4. Partially fund three requests on both banks. Replay independent local orders and vary subsequent delivery across the deadline. Compare exact committed intersections under the timely schedule, released reservations or authenticated absence and the three terminal accounting rows. [Model-side evidence](finalized-admission.md)
