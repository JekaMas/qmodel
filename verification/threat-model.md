# Threat model

Untrusted relayers may delay, drop, replay, fabricate or substitute evidence. A certificate must authenticate immutable finalized provenance and every descriptor/public binding. Outages and crash/restart do not rewrite history. Byzantine source quorum, arbitrary issuer destruction, verifier takeover, irreversible evidence loss and key compromise are explicit assumption perturbations. Transport, custody, validator, upgrade and disclosure keys are independent authorities. Buyer/seller identity is independent of institution hosting their eligible accounts.
